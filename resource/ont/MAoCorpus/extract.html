Introduction

MAon conceptualizes martial arts as a form of embodied knowledge and an instance of Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH).
MAon v1.2 expands this initial framework, moving beyond the technical documentation of Southern folkloric styles to incorporate generalizable entities that describe Northern and Southern Chinese martial arts. It incorporates traditional folk discursive habits with the structured, modernized theories synthesized from the Dictionary of Chinese Martial Arts (Zhongguo Wushu Dacidian, 1990) and the Record of Chinese Martial Arts Fist and Weapon Forms (Zhongguo Wushu Quanxie Lu, 1983).
MAon v1.1, focused extensively on technical characteristics and their underlying philosophical aspects, primarily based on the Hong Kong Martial Arts Living Archive. It also established mappings to CIDOC-CRM classes to represent sociocultural dimensions within a formalized semantic framework for heritage documentation.

Current version: 1.2

This release also includes the instantiation of Form_movements for both sides and their corresponding spars_against relations in Gung Gee Fok Fu Kuen Deui Chaak (Taming Tiger Boxing Two-Man Set), accessible through SQL queries in the Open Dataset Explorer.
-   Contributors: Jinghong Lin (ORCID), with advice from Yumeng HOU
Updates in v1.1: Refined terminology for improved conceptual accuracy. Specifically, Form has been renamed to Form_movement, with its associated object properties updated to in_form (previously is_form_in) and similar_movement_to (previously similar_form_to). Armament has been renamed to Weaponry to better align with the conventions of Asian martial arts studies, and MA_Clan has been introduced to better portray lineage and masters' networks.
-   Contributor: Yumeng HOU (ORCID)

MAon_Individuals: Annotated instances of core individuals and vocabularies, such as key technical concepts, weapons, and objects.

E74.Group↑ top

IRImao:E74
Has subclassesMA_Clan, MA_Organization
DescriptionThis class comprises any gatherings or organizations of human individuals or groups that act collectively or in a similar way due to any form of unifying relationship. In the wider sense this class also comprises official positions which used to be regarded in certain contexts as one actor, independent of the current holder of the office, such as the president of a country. In such cases, it may happen that the group never had more than one member. A joint pseudonym (i.e. a name that seems indicative of an individual but that is actually used as a persona by two or more people) is a particular case of E74 Group. A gathering of people becomes an instance of E74 Group when it exhibits organizational characteristics usually typified by a set of ideas or beliefs held in common, or actions performed together. These might be communication, creating some common artifact, a common purpose such as study, worship, business, sports, etc. Nationality can be modelled as membership in an instance of E74 Group. Married couples and other concepts of family are regarded as particular examples of E74 Group.

Form_movement 招式 Shi↑ top

IRImao:Form_move
Subclass ofTraining_method
DescriptionThe class Form_movement encapsulates the concept of shi. Shi signifies the individual units of movement within a training form, which is a fundamental aspect of technical and stylistic training in traditional martial arts. Examples of this class include zhaoshi (or shi) in Chinese terminology. An instance of form movement, whether static or dynamic, is a choreographed pattern of movement comprising a unitary sequence of basic techniques. It typically involves a movement that employs several techniques, simultaneously serving as a training tool for those techniques and the qualities relevant to fulfilling them. The property has component is used; it is designed to inherit the CIDOC-CRM declaration of P148 has component and to indicate the relationship between a form_movement and the technique(s) trained within.
Alt. names (EN)Form movement
Alt. names (ZH)式
